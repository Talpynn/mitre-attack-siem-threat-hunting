## 4. Use of Sources

MITRE ATT&CK was used to identify the group, campaign, and technique IDs.

CISA was used as a government incident-response and threat advisory source.

Mandiant's investigation was used for technical context about the SUNBURST backdoor and the SolarWinds supply-chain compromise.

Technique mappings in this report are based on publicly documented campaign behavior. They should not be interpreted as evidence that the same behavior occurred in the local laboratory.
